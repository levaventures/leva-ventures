# Leva Ventures — Accessibility Statement (Draft, Not Published)

Leva Ventures, LLC aims to make its website and Commercial Financing Intelligence calculator usable by people with disabilities, including people who use assistive technology, keyboard-only navigation, screen readers, or enlarged text.

## Measures taken
- Keyboard-operable loan and refinance calculator controls and capital-stack sliders.
- Text table providing the same financing breakdown as the capital-stack graphic.
- Optional spoken financial explanations, with controls to start and stop narration and read a transcript.
- Clear input labels, error messages and on-demand results summaries.
- Improved contrast and navigation labeling across the homepage and Insights articles.

## Standards and current testing status
Our target is Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA. During November 2026, we completed automated tests on the current development version and corrected detectable issues. The automated site-wide scan covered the homepage, Insights hub, three articles and both calculator modes, returning zero detected violations **within the rules tested**. We also carried out targeted tests with the NVDA screen reader on Windows.

These results do **not** certify full WCAG 2.2 Level AA conformance, and we do not claim an official ADA compliance certification. Some usability aspects, third-party services, and individual assistive-technology configurations may still require additional verification. The public version should be re-evaluated when changes are deployed.

## Feedback and help
If a feature is difficult to access, use the [Leva Ventures contact form](https://levaventures.com/#contact) to describe the issue, the page, the browser or assistive technology used, and the requested alternative. Do not submit sensitive personal or financial account information.

Leva Ventures will review accessibility feedback and work toward reasonable solutions. No specific response period is promised in this draft.

## Details
- Organization: Leva Ventures, LLC
- Intended standard: WCAG 2.2 Level AA
- Last development-version evaluation: November 2026
- Method: automated axe-core rules, browser keyboard and responsive tests, selected real NVDA screen-reader focus/heading tests
- Site: https://levaventures.com/

**Internal publication note:** This draft is not a legal statement of ADA compliance and should not be published as a conformance claim without review of public-site functionality, third-party contact form/Turnstile and all critical user workflows.
