UNDERSTANDING YOUR RESULTS

From commercial loan payments to financing constraints

A commercial real estate loan calculator estimates the periodic payment based on principal, interest rate, and amortization. Many commercial loans mature before the amortization period ends. The outstanding principal at that maturity is commonly called the balloon balance.

How do DSCR, LTV and debt yield work?

Debt service coverage ratio (DSCR) compares annual net operating income (NOI) with annual loan payments. Loan-to-value (LTV) compares debt with the estimated property value. Debt yield compares NOI with the loan amount. Different lenders may impose multiple constraints, and the most restrictive assumption can determine estimated proceeds.

How is a refinancing gap calculated?

Our model uses the lowest of the DSCR, LTV and debt-yield loan limits, then compares those potential proceeds with the existing loan payoff, estimated closing costs and required cash reserves. The difference, when positive, is the modeled funding shortfall.

Worked financing example

Consider an illustrative $5 million property producing $350,000 in annual NOI, with a $3.4 million loan payoff. At an 8% assumed rate, 25-year amortization, minimum 1.25x DSCR, maximum 70% LTV and minimum 9% debt yield, the income-based constraint may be tighter than the property-value limit. The free analyzer updates the actual calculation as you change these assumptions.

FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Is this calculator free?

Yes. The loan and refinancing calculators work without an account, email address or payment. Premium analysis is a planned, separate product.

Are the financing estimates lender-approved?

No. These are illustrations based solely on your inputs and selected assumptions. A lender may use different underwriting data and standards.

What is the difference between loan term and amortization?

The loan term determines the maturity date. Amortization determines how the scheduled payments reduce principal. A shorter loan term can create a remaining balloon balance.

Can refinancing require more equity?

Yes. If the modeled new loan proceeds are less than the payoff, fees and reserves, the calculator shows the estimated additional amount needed.

Can I use the calculator without voice narration?

Yes. Narration never starts automatically. All financial results, explanatory text and the capital-stack table can be read without using audio. Voice availability varies by browser and device.

Does my information get sent to Leva?

The free financial calculations run locally in your browser; entering calculator numbers does not send them to Leva Ventures. Optional narration uses the speech voices available on your device or browser. Some voice services may process the narration text online, depending on your selection and device. Information is submitted to Leva only if you separately use the opportunity inquiry form. See the Leva Ventures privacy notice and your browser or device voice-provider settings.
