Accessibility and inclusion

Access to Leva Ventures

Leva Ventures, LLC aims to make its website and free Commercial Financing Intelligence calculator useful and accessible to people who navigate with a keyboard, screen reader, enlarged text or other assistive technology.

Current evaluation status: We aim to align with Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA. Our development testing includes automated accessibility scans and selected real screen-reader checks. These tests do not constitute an official WCAG certification or a determination of legal ADA compliance. We continue to evaluate and improve accessibility.

Accessibility features

  • Keyboard-operated commercial financing calculator and capital-stack controls
  • Text tables that explain key figures shown in the graphical capital stack
  • Optional spoken explanations with start and stop controls and written transcripts
  • Accessible input labels, visible keyboard focus and results summaries
  • Improved contrast for navigation, article information and legal notices

Compatibility and limitations

We have run automated browser evaluations and selected NVDA screen-reader tests on the development version of the financing calculator. Available speech voices and narration behavior depend on the browser and device. Some speech providers may process narration online. Third-party components, including the contact-form verification service, may require additional evaluation. We do not guarantee that every browser or assistive-technology configuration is free of barriers.

Report an accessibility issue

If you cannot access information or complete a task, please use our contact form and write “Accessibility feedback” at the beginning of your message. Include the page or feature, the type of barrier and, if helpful, your browser or assistive technology. We will review accessibility reports and consider an accessible alternative where possible.

Please do not include Social Security numbers, passwords, bank details or other sensitive account information in an accessibility report.

Standards and testing

Our goal is to align with WCAG 2.2 Level AA. Our development-version checks have included automated WCAG rules and targeted screen-reader and keyboard tests. This statement reflects our intentions and the limitations of our current testing rather than a formal whole-site conformance claim.
