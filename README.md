# Leva Ventures — Financing Intelligence Desk

**A free, browser-based commercial real estate financing analysis tool from Leva Ventures, LLC.**

### [Launch the live Financing Intelligence Desk](https://levaventures.com/tools/financing-intelligence/)

The Desk helps commercial property owners, acquisition sponsors, and financing professionals explore **illustrative** debt capacity, refinancing shortfalls, and capital structure alternatives.

| Connected workspace | What it helps analyze |
| --- | --- |
| Debt Capacity | Illustrative senior financing ceilings under DSCR, LTV, and debt-yield tests |
| Loan & Balloon | Scheduled payments, amortization, and remaining debt at maturity |
| Refinance Intelligence | Modeled payoff, closing uses, financing proceeds, and potential funding gap |
| Capital Structure | Illustrative senior financing, supplemental capital, sponsor cash, and any remaining gap |
| Scenario Lab | How different NOI and interest assumptions affect financing capacity |
| Investment Returns | Illustrative investment cash flows and return measures |
| Equity Waterfall | Simplified LP/GP distribution assumptions |
| Analysis Report | A readable summary of selected modeled assumptions and results |

**No account is needed to use the calculator.** Its numeric financial inputs are calculated within the browser and are not automatically sent to Leva Ventures. Submitting a separate voluntary financing inquiry on the company website is an independent user action.

## Important limitations

Outputs are educational, assumption-based illustrations. They are not offers of financing, lender underwriting or approvals, confirmed capital availability, valuation opinions, investment advice, or assurances of future performance. Financing decisions require independent verification and consideration of actual lender requirements and deal documentation.
