## LinkedIn post 1 — launch (proposed owner/company post)

A property can have significant equity and still face a refinancing shortfall.

Take a hypothetical $8 million commercial property with $520,000 in annual underwritten NOI, an existing $5.6 million mortgage payoff, and $180,000 in costs and reserves.

At 7.50% interest, 25-year amortization, a 1.25x minimum DSCR, 65% maximum LTV and 9% minimum debt yield, the NOI-based DSCR limit supports approximately $4.69 million in senior debt. That leaves a modeled refinance funding gap of about $1.09 million.

The relevant question isn't only, “What's the property worth?” It's, “Which underwriting constraint determines proceeds, and how much cash must be addressed at closing?”

We developed Leva's free Financing Intelligence Desk to help property owners, brokers and sponsors explore those questions across eight connected workspaces. There is no registration and the calculator's financial inputs stay in the browser.

https://levaventures.com/go/linkedin/

Illustrative assumptions only, not lender terms or a financing offer.

## LinkedIn post 2 — practitioner education (space posts; don't duplicate launch)

Why does the LTV calculation say a refinance should work when the lender's loan sizing says otherwise?

Because debt capacity is often the minimum of multiple constraints: DSCR, LTV and debt yield.

That gap between appraised collateral value and supportable income can become particularly important around maturity, lease-up or an acquisition.

We've published a practical guide and free analysis tool for sponsors who want to pressure-test scenarios before entering a lender discussion.

https://levaventures.com/go/linkedin/

Education, not a lender quote or promise of capital.
