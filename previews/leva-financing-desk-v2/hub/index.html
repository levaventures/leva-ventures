Why does a property with strong value still have a refinance gap?

A loan may be constrained by cash flow, loan-to-value limits or minimum debt yield. When debt service coverage is binding, a higher hypothetical appraisal alone cannot increase supportable proceeds. Conversely, when LTV binds, improving NOI does not automatically produce a larger loan. The binding test matters more than a single headline valuation.

Do the tools produce lender quotes?

No. Rates, thresholds, property income and values are assumptions you control, not verified underwriting criteria, funding commitments or offers. Supplemental debt and preferred-equity illustrations are not representations of available capital.

How are investment IRR and cash-flow timing handled?

The investment model uses year-end equity cash flows and a periodic annual IRR calculation. Cash-flow timing, taxes, capital expenditures, lender covenants and legal distributions can materially alter actual outcomes. The simplified waterfall does not replace an operating agreement.

What happens to the financial inputs?

In this preview, calculations run in the browser. The tool does not submit or retain financial model inputs. Locally generated downloads and print-to-PDF output are controlled through the browser, and individual browser or printing services may have their own settings.
