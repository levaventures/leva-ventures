Methodology and limitations

The lowest of DSCR, LTV and debt-yield ceilings controls modeled senior proceeds. Monthly debt service uses nominal annual interest divided by 12 and equal end-of-month amortizing payments. Cash gap equals the positive difference between total closing uses and senior proceeds. Results are not underwritten offers, commitments, investment advice or approved financing terms.
